## Executive Summary

Describe user impact, duration, scope, and current state without speculation.

## Impact

Quantify affected users, requests, data, objectives, cost, and business operations. State unknowns.

## Detection

Explain the first reliable signal, who or what noticed it, and detection gaps.

## Mitigation

## Root Cause

Explain the causal mechanism and triggering conditions. Do not stop at the component that emitted the error.

## Contributing Conditions

Document technical and organizational conditions that increased probability, impact, or recovery time without assigning blame.

## Recovery and Verification

State how user-visible recovery, data integrity, and system stability were proved.

## What Went Well

## What Made This Hard
