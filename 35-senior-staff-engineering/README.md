# 35 — Senior and Staff Engineering

Senior and staff engineering is the practice of improving technical outcomes beyond the work one person can implement directly.

## What you will learn

- Frame ambiguous problems and make decisions legible.
- Lead technical change across teams without relying on authority.
- Build durable systems, ownership, and engineering judgment.

## Lessons

1. [Scope, judgment, and decisions](01-scope-judgment-and-decisions.md)
2. [Influence and technical leadership](02-influence-and-technical-leadership.md)
3. [Stewardship and durable impact](03-stewardship-and-durable-impact.md)

## Practice

Write a decision record for a consequential change, map stakeholders and risks, then define how ownership and evidence will survive your direct involvement.

## Ready to continue

You can turn ambiguity into bounded work, align people through evidence, and leave systems and teams more capable than you found them.
