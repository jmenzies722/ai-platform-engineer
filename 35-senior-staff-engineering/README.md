# 35 — Senior and Staff Engineering

Senior and staff engineering is the practice of improving important technical outcomes beyond the work one person can implement directly. The title grants neither clarity nor influence; both are earned through judgment, mechanisms, and durable service to the organization.

## What you will learn

- Choose scope from organizational outcomes rather than ticket volume.
- Turn ambiguity into strategy, technical vision, decisions, and executable boundaries.
- Influence across teams while preserving dissent and accountable ownership.
- Lead execution, risk, incidents, mentoring, and interfaces without becoming the permanent bottleneck.
- Steward systems, standards, and people with ethical and AI-platform judgment.
