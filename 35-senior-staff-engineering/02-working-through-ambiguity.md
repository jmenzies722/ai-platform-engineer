# Working through ambiguity

Ambiguity is not permission to invent certainty; it is a signal to make unknowns, values, and decision ownership explicit.

## Why it matters

Cross-cutting work stalls when people argue from different problem definitions or demand evidence for a conflict that is actually about priorities and consequences.

## How it works

Frame the outcome, affected users, current state, constraints, invariants, non-goals, and decision required. Separate facts, forecasts, assumptions, preferences, and policies because each is resolved differently. Facts can be measured, forecasts bounded, assumptions tested, preferences negotiated, and policies interpreted by accountable authority.

Build an uncertainty map using consequence and reducibility. Run the smallest discriminating experiment for high-consequence uncertainty that evidence can reduce. For irreversible or externally binding choices, raise the evidence bar. Time-box discovery and define what evidence permits a decision; “more research” is not a plan.

Progressive clarity means the next commitment is precise even when the final plan is not. Publish what is known, unknown, decided, and next. Do not absorb a value conflict into technical language. If product speed and regulatory caution conflict, the authorized risk owner must decide after engineering makes consequences legible.

## See it yourself

Rewrite “standardize our AI stack” as a measurable problem: six teams duplicate provider authentication, audit, quotas, and evaluation; two incidents came from inconsistent controls; migration must not block experimentation. This framing permits evidence and alternatives without assuming a platform is the answer.

## Where it shows up

Ambiguity dominates platform mandates, acquisitions, regulatory changes, cost emergencies, and new AI capabilities. Staff engineers create enough structure for parallel learning while keeping the decision boundary visible.

## When it breaks

Failure modes include analysis theater, a solution disguised as a requirement, private assumptions, and escalation used to avoid recommendation. When work loops, publish the unresolved claim, evidence, consequence, owner, and decision date.

## Practice

**Build:** frame an ambiguous “AI platform” request with five uncertainty classes, experiments, decision owners, and a two-stage commitment. **Break:** reveal that teams disagree about autonomy rather than technology. **Explain back:** identify what engineering can prove and what leadership must choose.

## Check yourself

1. Which uncertainties can an experiment resolve?
2. What makes a next step precise without pretending the destination is known?
3. When is escalation the honest technical action?
